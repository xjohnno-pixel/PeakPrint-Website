## 2. What personal information we collect

We collect the following information directly from you when you place an order or contact us:

- **Identity & contact:** full name, email address, delivery address, phone number (if provided)
- **Order information:** product configuration (frame colour, tile colour, add-ons), adventure title, subtitle, distance, elevation, location, date of event, time completed
- **Activity / GPS data:** the route data file you upload (`.gpx`, `.kml`, `.fit`, `.tcx`), or a link to a publicly accessible activity (e.g. Strava, AllTrails, Garmin, Komoot), or a screenshot image
- **Payment information:** processed directly by our payment provider — we do not store your full card details on our servers
- **Correspondence and communications:** any information you provide when you contact us through any channel, including emails, SMS or other text messages, phone calls, voicemails, social media messages and direct messages (for example via Instagram), website contact or order form submissions, and in-person conversations during order collection. This includes the content of your message, your contact details, the channel used, and the date and time. We do not record phone calls. Any correspondence you initiate is retained alongside your order or enquiry record so we can keep an accurate history of our interactions with you.

We do **not** collect information about you from third parties without your knowledge.

## 3. How we collect it

We collect information when you:

- Place an order through our website
- Email us at hello@peakframed.com.au
- Send us a route link or upload a route file as part of your order

## 4. Why we collect it

We collect your information to:

- Produce, package and deliver your custom map
- Communicate with you about your order
- Respond to your enquiries
- Issue invoices and receipts
- Comply with our legal obligations (tax records, consumer law)
- Improve our products and service
- Send abandoned-cart reminder emails (only with your consent — see section 4a below)

We do **not** sell or rent your information for marketing.

## 4a. Marketing communications and abandoned-cart emails

If you start an order on our website but do not complete checkout, we may send you a small number of **abandoned-cart reminder emails** to help you finish your purchase. These emails are sent through Shopify on our behalf.

You will only receive these emails if you have provided your email address and consented to marketing communications during checkout. You can withdraw consent at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link in any marketing email, or by emailing hello@peakframed.com.au.

These emails are sent in line with the **Spam Act 2003 (Cth)** — they will always identify Peak Framed as the sender, and will always include a working unsubscribe option.

We do not currently send a regular newsletter. If we begin doing so in the future, we will require separate explicit consent before adding you to that list.

## 5. Who we share it with

We share your information only with third parties necessary to fulfil your order:

- **E-commerce platform:** Shopify (which processes your order details, including name and delivery address)
- **Payment processor:** Shopify Payments
- **Shipping carrier:** Australia Post
- **File hosting (route data uploads):** Cloudinary (servers may be located outside Australia — see section 9)
- **Email service:** CheaperDomains (hosts our hello@peakframed.com.au mailbox on their own mail servers)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to anyone.

## 6. How we store and protect it

Your information is stored on secure servers operated by our service providers. We take reasonable steps — including encryption in transit (HTTPS), restricted access, and regular review — to protect your information from misuse, loss, unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure.

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfil your order and meet our legal obligations (typically **7 years** for tax and consumer-law records, in line with the Australian Taxation Office's record-keeping requirements). After that, your personal information is securely deleted or de-identified.

GPS route files may be retained beyond this period in case you re-order, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

## 7. Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you
- **Correct** any information that is inaccurate, out of date, or incomplete
- **Withdraw consent** to our use of your information at any time (subject to our ability to fulfil any active order)
- **Make a complaint** about how we have handled your information

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@peakframed.com.au. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are unhappy with our response, you may contact the **Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC)** at oaic.gov.au or 1300 363 992.

## 8. Cookies and website analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

- Remember your selections (frame colour, tile colour, etc.)
- Understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it

We use **Shopify Analytics** (the built-in analytics provided by Shopify) to understand site traffic, popular products, and conversion. Shopify Analytics may set first-party cookies and collects information such as the pages you visit, the device and browser you use, and your approximate location. This data is processed by Shopify on our behalf and is not sold or shared with third parties.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Some site features may not work properly if you do.

## 9. Cross-border disclosure

By using our service, you consent to this transfer. We take reasonable steps to ensure these providers handle your information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.
