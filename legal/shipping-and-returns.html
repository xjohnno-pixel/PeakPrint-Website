Shipping & Returns Policy

Shipping

Production time

Each Peak Framed map is custom-made from your GPS data. Production typically takes 1–3 business days from order confirmation. During peak periods (e.g. Black Friday, Christmas, Mother's / Father's Day), please allow up to 8 business days for production.

Delivery

  • Standard delivery within Australia is included in the product price.
  • We ship via Australia Post.
  • Delivery time after dispatch is typically 3–7 business days depending on your location.
  • We will email you a tracking number once your order ships.

Collect in person

Collection in person is available from our two Melbourne pickup points:

  • Port Melbourne (inner-west)
  • Knoxfield (outer-east)

You can choose your preferred pickup location at checkout. We will email you when your order is ready to confirm the exact pickup address and an agreed collection time.

Collection is not available outside the Melbourne metropolitan area. If you are based elsewhere — including regional Victoria or interstate — please select standard delivery instead.

Gifting

Because each Peak Framed map is built from highly personal GPS data, we recommend purchasing a gift card instead of ordering a surprise map for someone else. This way the recipient can supply their own route and personalise the design. See section 3a of our Terms of Service for full details.

Bulk orders

For orders of more than 10 maps in one transaction, please contact us first at hello@peakframed.com.au so we can coordinate timing and shipping.

International shipping

We do not currently ship outside Australia. If you would like to discuss international delivery, please email hello@peakframed.com.au.

Lost or delayed parcels

If your order has not arrived within 20 business days of dispatch, please contact us and we will investigate with Australia Post.

Damaged in transit

If your order arrives damaged, please email hello@peakframed.com.au within 5 business days of delivery with:

  • Your order number
  • Photos of the damaged packaging and product

We will work with you on a resolution. You do not need to ship the damaged product back to us — clear photographs are sufficient. This saves you a trip to the post office and helps us process your claim faster.

Returns and refunds

Custom-made products

Because every Peak Framed map is made to order from your GPS data and personal details, we cannot accept returns for change of mind and we cannot offer refunds for incorrect information you supplied (e.g. wrong adventure title, wrong route, wrong delivery address).

Please double-check your order details before submitting.

Faulty or incorrect products

If your product is:

  • Faulty (e.g. broken frame, printing defect)
  • Significantly different from what was described
  • Sent to you in error

…contact us at hello@peakframed.com.au within 5 business days of delivery and we will work with you on a resolution.

Your rights under Australian Consumer Law

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies your rights under the Australian Consumer Law (Schedule 2 of the Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth)). You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure, and to compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage.

Cancellations

You can request to cancel your order before production begins for a full refund. Once production has started, a partial fee may apply to cover work already completed. We will confirm by email when production starts.
